Reading the comparison

Conditions & provenance

Download case data (CSV) ↓

These are selected qualitative examples of Ours reaching the goal while all three baseline rollouts end in a collision or road departure. They do not estimate aggregate performance. All six cases use traffic density 0.3.

Within each case, the road geometry, map seed, traffic seed and complete initial vehicle geometry match across methods. Background traffic remains interactive and can respond differently to each policy. The number of vehicles in the camera view changes as traffic moves through the map.

Each panel follows its own ego vehicle using the same camera specification. The real terminal frame is held after a rollout ends. Failure and success labels appear at the respective terminal times; Ours includes its complete route to arrival. Playback is 5× simulation time.

Replay and training-seed details

The visualizations use native MetaDrive/Panda3D road and vehicle assets, rendered from recorded vehicle states. Sparse state samples are interpolated for display only. Unrecorded visual properties, including material colors, wheel angles and vehicle pitch, use native defaults.

Fixed 50K models are used throughout. Cases 02 and 06 include different training seeds across methods; the map and traffic seeds still match. Per-method seeds, outcomes and terminal simulation times are included in the case data.

Ours passed the saved-action, physics-step, transition-event and safe-arrival checks for every displayed case. Cases 01, 02, 05 and 06 retain a failed strict numerical replay status because rewards or completion values drifted; cases 03 and 04 passed that numerical check. The videos therefore document verified qualitative outcomes, without claiming every logged scalar was reproduced exactly.
