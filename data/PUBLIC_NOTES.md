# Video case notes

These are six selected qualitative examples in MetaDrive at configured traffic density 0.3: five roundabouts and one intersection. They were selected for safe arrival by Ours and collision or departure from the road by each of the three baselines. They are not a random sample or an estimate of overall performance. The displayed replay outcomes comprise 17 baseline collisions, one baseline departure from the road, and six safe arrivals by Ours; no timeout is counted as a failure.

Within each case, methods share the map seed, traffic seed, map geometry, and full initial vehicle geometry (positions, headings, lengths and widths). Background traffic remains interactive and can react differently to each ego policy after reset. Matching the initial world does not imply identical later traffic trajectories. Initial background counts are 43–66 over the full world, not the number visible in the local camera.

All policies use 50K training-transition checkpoints. Cases 02 and 06 mix policy training seeds across methods; these seeds are listed explicitly in cases.json and cases.csv. The other cases use one common training seed within the case. Scenario matching is independent of policy training seed.

Baselines are genuine frozen-checkpoint simulator replays. Ours is a physical simulator reconstruction using the archived executed skills. In all six Ours cases, full-episode, safe-arrival, initial-observation, executed-action, physics-step-count, transition-event and terminal-event checks pass. Original numerical verification remains FAIL in cases 01, 02, 05 and 06, and PASS in cases 03 and 04: rewards and route-completion values drift in cases 01, 02 and 05; rewards drift in case 06. Cost values match in all six. These statuses are retained in the public case data; the recordings do not establish equality of every logged quantity with the archived evaluation. Some baseline replay terminal causes also differ from archived records; the displayed labels and case data refer to the actual shown replay outcomes.

Videos use native MetaDrive 3D rendering from recorded physical vehicle states. Sparse saved states are interpolated for display only; stored states and terminal timestamps are preserved. Unrecorded materials and other visual attributes use native defaults. Each panel follows its own trajectory, shows its own actual terminal event, and holds that frame afterward. Ours is shown through safe arrival. These are not screenshots in which every baseline fails at the same instant. Playback is 5×; terminal_times_s gives simulation seconds, while duration_s includes video titles and terminal holds.

Panel order is SAC (upper left), SAC-Lag (upper right), CPO (lower left), and Ours (lower right). Outcome fields use collision, out_of_road, and success. No author names, manuscript title, or citation metadata are asserted by this case package.
